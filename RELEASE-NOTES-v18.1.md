# WoundAI v18.1 — Guided clinical workflow

- Saving a new patient automatically continues to wound registration.
- Saving a wound automatically opens imaging and selects the patient and wound.
- Saving an image automatically advances to clinical assessment.
- Saving a clinical assessment automatically advances to care planning.
- Saving a care-plan draft or clinician-approved plan advances to reports.
- Patient and wound selections are carried between workflow stages.
- Dialogs close automatically on successful save; no manual dismissal is required to continue.
- Validation errors do not advance the workflow.
- Existing navigation remains available for returning to prior stages or working out of sequence.
